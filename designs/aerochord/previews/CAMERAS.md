# Preview cameras

Frozen shots for the product page and review rounds. Cameras are fixed once a
reviewer has seen them — add a new `cameras.conf` line for a new region rather
than moving an existing camera, so before/after images line up.

- **contact-sheet** — the standard 2×2 (iso / top / front / bottom-iso) of the
  printable solid; bottom-iso is for checking overhangs and bed contact.
- **air-path** — longitudinal cutaway through the middle voice, exposing the
  full novel air path in one view: mouthpiece → plenum → windway → flue exit →
  open window → labium → closed-pipe bore with its self-supporting conical top.
- **fipple** — close-up of the same cutaway at the base, showing the fipple in
  detail: the thin windway, the flue exit, the open window (mouth), and the
  beveled labium with its printable land.
